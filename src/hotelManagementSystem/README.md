Problem Statement

Design a Hotel Management System that allows hotel staff to manage room bookings, guest information, and various hotel services.  The system should support the following operations:
 1. Room management (different types, pricing, availability)
 2. Guest registration and management
 3. Booking and reservation handling
 4. Check-in and check-out processes
 5. Billing and payment processing
 6. Hotel services management (room service, housekeeping, etc.)
 7. Search and reporting functionality

Requirements

The system should manage different room types (standard, deluxe, suite) with different prices and capacities
The system should track room availability and status (available, booked, occupied, under maintenance)
Guests should be able to make, modify, and cancel reservations
The system should handle check-in and check-out processes including key card management
The system should generate and manage bills including room charges and additional services
The system should track and manage hotel services like room service, housekeeping, and concierge
The system should generate reports on occupancy rates, revenue, and guest statistics


Constraints

A room can only be occupied by one reservation at a time
Reservations require valid guest information and payment details
Cancellation policies apply based on how close to the reservation date the cancellation occurs
Check-in is not allowed before the standard check-in time unless early check-in is arranged
Check-out after the standard check-out time may incur additional charges
